Privacy Policy — EVERFEST

Last updated: 1 August 2026 · Everbest Games

EVERFEST does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

What data we collect

None. EVERFEST requires no account and no sign-in. The game works fully offline and makes no network connections.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — such as your festival layout, cash, career progress and unlocks — is saved locally on your device using standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Third parties, ads and analytics

EVERFEST contains no advertising, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

EVERFEST collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts processing data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? The contact address is in the Impressum.
